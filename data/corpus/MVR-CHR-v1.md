# Market Risk Value-at-Risk Model — Model Charter

MODEL CHARTER — SUPERSEDED
The Market Risk Value-at-Risk Model is used to estimate one-day 99 percent value at risk for the trading portfolio. Model version 6.6 is owned by Lucas Martin in Market Risk Management. It is classified Tier 1. The operating decision threshold is 2.40 million USD. This charter was effective from 2025-01-15 through 2025-09-30 and was replaced by CHR-v2. Do not use this version for decisions on or after 2025-10-01.
