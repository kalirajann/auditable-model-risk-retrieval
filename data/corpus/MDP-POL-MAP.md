# Mortgage Default and Prepayment Model — Policy Mapping

POLICY CONTROL MAPPING
The Mortgage Default and Prepayment Model is Tier 2 and is subject to a risk-based governance approach. Accountable business unit: Mortgage Portfolio Risk. Required control: quarterly calibration monitoring and annual independent validation. Independent validation must remain separate from model ownership. Governance mapping references the 2026 revised interagency model-risk guidance and NIST AI RMF for general AI governance. Generative AI is governed by bank policy even though the 2026 banking guidance states that generative and agentic AI are outside that guidance's scope.
