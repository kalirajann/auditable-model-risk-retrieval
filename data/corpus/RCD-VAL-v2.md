# Retail Credit Default Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — CURRENT
Validation of approved version 3.0 reported area under the ROC curve (AUC) of 0.804. Independent testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, outcomes analysis, and review of use. The remaining limitation is that thin-file applicants are underrepresented in the development sample. Validation approved use subject to quarterly performance monitoring and annual independent validation. This report supersedes RCD-VAL-v1.
