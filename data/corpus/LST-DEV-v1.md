# Liquidity Stress Testing Model — Development Report

DEVELOPMENT REPORT
Purpose: estimate 30-day stressed cash outflows under bank-specific and market-wide scenarios. Development data consist of aggregated deposit flows, secured funding positions, and scenario variables from 2019-2025. Records were de-identified or synthetic before modeling. Time-based sampling was used: earlier observations for development and later observations for holdout testing. The report documents transformations, missing-value rules, feature definitions, and reproducible code parameters. Known limitation: digital-channel deposit runoff during severe idiosyncratic stress has limited history. No customer names, account numbers, or raw protected-attribute values are retained in this benchmark record.
