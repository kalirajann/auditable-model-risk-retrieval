# Card Transaction Fraud Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — CURRENT
Validation of approved version 4.4 reported precision at the review-capacity cutoff of 0.713. Independent testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, outcomes analysis, and review of use. The remaining limitation is that new-merchant patterns have shorter label histories than established merchants. Validation approved use subject to monthly drift monitoring and semiannual independent validation. This report supersedes CTF-VAL-v1.
