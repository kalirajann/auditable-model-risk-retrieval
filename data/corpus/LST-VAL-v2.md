# Liquidity Stress Testing Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — CURRENT
Validation of approved version 3.5 reported back-test ratio of realized to forecast stressed outflows of 0.97. Independent testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, outcomes analysis, and review of use. The remaining limitation is that digital-channel deposit runoff during severe idiosyncratic stress has limited history. Validation approved use subject to monthly back-testing and annual independent validation. This report supersedes LST-VAL-v1.
