# Market Risk Value-at-Risk Model — Development Report

DEVELOPMENT REPORT
Purpose: estimate one-day 99 percent value at risk for the trading portfolio. Development data consist of daily trading positions and market-risk factors from 2017-2025. Records were de-identified or synthetic before modeling. Time-based sampling was used: earlier observations for development and later observations for holdout testing. The report documents transformations, missing-value rules, feature definitions, and reproducible code parameters. Known limitation: basis risk for less-liquid municipal instruments is represented by proxy series. No customer names, account numbers, or raw protected-attribute values are retained in this benchmark record.
