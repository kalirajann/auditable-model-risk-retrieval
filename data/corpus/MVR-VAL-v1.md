# Market Risk Value-at-Risk Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — SUPERSEDED
Validation of version 6.6 reported rolling 250-day back-testing exceptions of 3 exceptions. Testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, and outcomes analysis. The principal limitation was that basis risk for less-liquid municipal instruments is represented by proxy series. This validation was superseded by MVR-VAL-v2 on 2025-11-15.
