# Retail Credit Default Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — SUPERSEDED
Validation of version 2.3 reported area under the ROC curve (AUC) of 0.781. Testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, and outcomes analysis. The principal limitation was that thin-file applicants are underrepresented in the development sample. This validation was superseded by RCD-VAL-v2 on 2025-11-15.
