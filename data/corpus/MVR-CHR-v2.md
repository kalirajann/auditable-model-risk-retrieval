# Market Risk Value-at-Risk Model — Model Charter

MODEL CHARTER — CURRENT
The Market Risk Value-at-Risk Model is used to estimate one-day 99 percent value at risk for the trading portfolio. Approved model version 7.0 is owned by Sofia Alvarez in Market Risk Management. It is classified Tier 1. The current operating decision threshold is 2.10 million USD. This charter supersedes MVR-CHR-v1 effective 2025-10-01. Required control: daily exception monitoring and annual independent validation.
