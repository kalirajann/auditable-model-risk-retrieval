# Liquidity Stress Testing Model — Model Charter

MODEL CHARTER — SUPERSEDED
The Liquidity Stress Testing Model is used to estimate 30-day stressed cash outflows under bank-specific and market-wide scenarios. Model version 3.2 is owned by Henry Chen in Treasury Risk. It is classified Tier 1. The operating decision threshold is 1.12. This charter was effective from 2025-01-15 through 2025-09-30 and was replaced by CHR-v2. Do not use this version for decisions on or after 2025-10-01.
