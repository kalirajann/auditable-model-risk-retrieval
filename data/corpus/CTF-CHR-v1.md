# Card Transaction Fraud Model — Model Charter

MODEL CHARTER — SUPERSEDED
The Card Transaction Fraud Model is used to rank card transactions by likelihood of fraud for analyst review. Model version 4.1 is owned by Priya Shah in Payments Fraud Operations. It is classified Tier 1. The operating decision threshold is 0.81. This charter was effective from 2025-01-15 through 2025-09-30 and was replaced by CHR-v2. Do not use this version for decisions on or after 2025-10-01.
