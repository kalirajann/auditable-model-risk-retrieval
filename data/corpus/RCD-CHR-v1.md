# Retail Credit Default Model — Model Charter

MODEL CHARTER — SUPERSEDED
The Retail Credit Default Model is used to estimate 12-month probability of default for unsecured retail loans. Model version 2.3 is owned by Elena Brooks in Consumer Lending Risk. It is classified Tier 1. The operating decision threshold is 0.62. This charter was effective from 2025-01-15 through 2025-09-30 and was replaced by CHR-v2. Do not use this version for decisions on or after 2025-10-01.
