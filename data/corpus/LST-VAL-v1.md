# Liquidity Stress Testing Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — SUPERSEDED
Validation of version 3.2 reported back-test ratio of realized to forecast stressed outflows of 0.93. Testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, and outcomes analysis. The principal limitation was that digital-channel deposit runoff during severe idiosyncratic stress has limited history. This validation was superseded by LST-VAL-v2 on 2025-11-15.
