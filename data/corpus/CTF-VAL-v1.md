# Card Transaction Fraud Model — Independent Validation

INDEPENDENT VALIDATION — SUPERSEDED
Validation of version 4.1 reported precision at the review-capacity cutoff of 0.684. Testing covered conceptual soundness, implementation verification, sensitivity analysis, and outcomes analysis. The principal limitation was that new-merchant patterns have shorter label histories than established merchants. This validation was superseded by CTF-VAL-v2 on 2025-11-15.
